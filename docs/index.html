A NOTE FROM THE LAB / 001

Not another agent waiting for instructions. A team that can think together.

Fellows is an open-source project investigating peer-first collaboration between autonomous agents. The idea is simple to say and difficult to get right: agents should be able to share what they know, question each other's decisions, organise work, and move toward a common result—while reliable infrastructure keeps state, permissions, and execution in check.

We're at the design and research stage. The runtime is not built yet. For now, we're working through the architecture and figuring out what a fair, measurable first experiment should look like.
